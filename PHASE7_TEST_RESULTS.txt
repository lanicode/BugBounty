DIAGNOSTIC RUNS
---------------

The first full-suite run in the restricted sandbox could not bind twelve
loopback test servers and reported listen EPERM 127.0.0.1. The other 402 tests
passed. The exact unchanged suite was rerun with local socket permission and
passed 78/78 files and 414/414 tests. No assertion or test was disabled.

Early Chromium diagnostics failed closed first on the Demo CSP and then on a
76-pixel instead of viewport-height mask. The final implementation constructs
and verifies a CSP-compatible full-viewport mask and retains the original CSP.
No raw screenshot survived these diagnostics. One initial format check found
demo-response.ts unformatted; Prettier corrected it and the final check passed.
